A light, friendly tool that turns study habits into a clear performance forecast — in seconds.

How it works

Three simple frames — from raw habits to a readable forecast.

1

Enter the habits

Study hours, attendance, previous scores, sleep and assignment completion — the everyday signals that actually move a grade.

2

Weighted analysis

Each factor is scored and weighted — attendance and past performance carry more weight than any single habit alone.

3

Readable forecast

A predicted score, a letter grade and a risk level — plus which factor helped or hurt the most.

Try the predictor

Heuristic model — a weighted formula, not trained on real data. Great for exploring what-if scenarios.
